Engineering Case Study · Ecosystem Enablement

Intel Sensor Catalog Program

Helping sensor vendors enable solutions on Intel architecture — and helping PC OEMs/ODMs evaluate sensing function/compatibility in project design.

Robin Wu · Platform & Ecosystem Engineering

1Challenge

PC OEM/ODM had to invest a lot of resource in selecting suitable sensing solution and driving sensor vendors to pass each OEM/ODM proprietary certification requirements.

2What I Did

I designed the Intel Sensor Catalog Program to support sensor vendors developing their products on Intel architecture and to simplify sensor evaluation for PC customers.

01

Collaborated with AAEON to develop an Intel reference board for enabling various sensor functions.

02

Reviewed OEM/ODM proprietary certification requirements and worked with Intel Sensor R&D to deliver development documentation and certification rules.

03

Established and managed vendor collaboration processes covering technical requirements, certification, and classification.

04

Regularly organized training, workshops, and demonstrations to support vendor and ecosystem enablement.

3For Whom

PC OEMs / ODMsSensor Vendors

4Results & Impact

Results · What Was Delivered

Built a sensor catalog to support PC OEM/ODM BOM/AVL selection of sensing solutions compatible with Intel platforms.

Updated the catalog for each Intel SoC generation.

Impact · Why It Mattered

Lowered the integration barriers for third-party sensor solutions on Intel architecture.

Strengthened Intel SoC competitiveness in PC OEM/ODM platform selection and business opportunities.

Key Lesson Learned

Building a scalable ecosystem requires more than enabling individual sensors. Reusable reference hardware, clear certification criteria, structured vendor processes, and ongoing partner education help turn diverse third-party solutions into practical choices for PC manufacturers.
